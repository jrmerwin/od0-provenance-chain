# Maturation, Geometry, and Duality in the Distinction Combinatorial Universe <br/>
### A throttled constructor, its mature random ideal, the 3-adic geometry of the record, and five sealed-holdout comparisons <br/>
Jason Merwin <br/>
Independent researcher <br/>
September 2026 <br/>
Abstract <br/> 
We report a preregistered, theorem-first derivation of the global maturation dynamics of
the Distinction Combinatorial Universe (DCU, formerly the Distinction Engine Universe)
from its frozen local equation system, and of the geometry that the resulting process supports.
Prior work established an exact, externally controlled local event law (OD0 rounds R0–
R47) and a combinatorial descent of its quantum-side scaffold; it did not establish an
autonomous origin-to-maturity process, and its historical simulations had reported strong
epoch dependence that the equations could not represent. Starting from a source census
that found the native constructor to be a global state space lacking only an opportunity
law, we classify the parameter-free opportunity laws, prove that the synchronous canonical
family saturates every registered ledger by its second step, and identify a unique minimal
throttle premise, TG1—serviced vacuum maintenance is enablement-active—under which
the constructor becomes a self-limiting Markov process. Conditional on a tower of stated
premises over the frozen local laws, we prove: an exact cost law for rendering a distinction
(paths through its recorded ancestry cone); a growth law N 2 ln N = (Γ + H − m)k/2; an
almost-sure frozen-support theorem, under which the mature universe is a nondegenerate
random ideal of the universal pair-closure DAG with inclusion probability O(Γ2/D) for a
structure available at size D; an absolute capacity spectrum for reachable semantic content;
a termination dichotomy at the critical load mc = Γ + min(H, 2Γ); the identity SV = ΓΦ2
making the operational lapse and the clock tick rate one observable; and a balance-band law
giving polynomial decay of the mean-square vacuum fraction. On geometry we prove a no-go:
no parameter-free gate or bounded-depth hierarchical measure on pair formation yields a
stable volume-growth exponent on the object DAG (ball growth is exponential with base
exactly 8 under uniform pairing), while the recorded universe carries an exact ultrametric of
3-adic dimension one whose boundary is the Pontryagin dual of the character-clock tower.
Under a further premise, TD1, the frozen specification’s regional ledgers instantiate a lapse
field on that geometry with a permanently free root region and a congestion boundary
d∗(N ) = log3(N/Γ). Five historical corpora sealed before the derivation began were opened
under preregistered protocols; none contradicted a theorem-grade prediction, and every
gap lay in observables the derivation had not frozen. The derived qutrit correlations agree
algebraically with standard finite-dimensional theory under the only dictionary the program
possesses.
Two things are not shown. The constructor’s parameter-free structure cannot produce a
spatial geometry, a length, or a physical time: this is a theorem, not a limitation of effort.
And no dictionary from the tower’s dimensionless invariants to physical units exists; the
bridge to empirical physics remains open, and this paper measures the gap rather than
closing it.
